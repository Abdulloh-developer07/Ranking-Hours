How We Handle Messages

Here's what happens after you get in touch, and how we keep things fair and private.

1. How We Handle Incoming Messages

Whether it's a reader, a manufacturer, or a developer reaching out, every message goes through the same process. This keeps things fair - a claim about a product's performance still has to match what we actually measured in our own testing.

If you send us raw test data - clock logs, thermal readings, color calibration results, that kind of thing - we check it over before a person on our team reviews it manually.

The Importance of Quantitative Communications

If you're reaching out about a testing dispute, skip the marketing talk - just send the actual numbers (model number, software version, temperature readings, etc). It helps us sort things out much faster.

That's true whether it's about an EV motor or a laptop chip - any correction to our published ratings has to be backed by real numbers, not just an opinion.

2. Disputing a Result

We keep our test conditions visible so anyone can check our numbers. If a brand thinks a score is off - say, a battery drain number looks too high - we're happy to look into it, since we know software updates really can change how a device behaves.

If a firmware update genuinely changed how a product performs, we'll schedule a retest so our published score stays accurate.

Managing Testing Deviations Collaboratively

When someone disputes a result, we go back and check our exact test conditions - room temperature, power supply, background processes running - to figure out why our number might differ from a manufacturer's own benchmark.

We'd rather compare notes on the actual data than argue over marketing claims - it keeps our numbers reliable for everyone reading the site.

3. Privacy & Data Handling

Anything you send us is kept private and isn't indexed by search engines - it's only seen by the people on our team who need to look at it.

This matters especially for pre-release products or confidential test data - it keeps details from leaking before a review is published.

Maintaining Industrial-Grade Integrity

Only team members working on a specific category can see files related to it, which helps keep any proprietary hardware details secure.

Whatever you send us is used only for the review it was meant for - never resold or reused for anything else.

4. Ads & Site Policies

We keep our advertising completely separate from our testing and scoring, so ads never influence what we publish.

We keep our ads.txt file up to date, which helps prevent ad fraud and keeps ads on our site legitimate.

Ensuring Global Accessibility

We aim to keep the site accessible to everyone, even during traffic spikes around big product launches.

We also try to keep pages fast and easy to read on both desktop and mobile.
