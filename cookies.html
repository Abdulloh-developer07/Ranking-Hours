When you first load the site, you'll see a cookie consent banner. Continuing to browse means you accept the cookies described below. If you don't agree, you can adjust your browser settings or leave the site.

2. How Cookies Work

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (PC, laptop, tablet, or phone) by your browser - Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, and so on. They're just text, not executable code - they can't run programs or read private files on your device.

A cookie assigns an anonymous ID to your browser, so our server can recognize you on return visits and remember your settings. Without cookies, something like your dark/light theme choice would reset every time you reload the page.

2.1 Types of Cookies

We use a few types of cookies, based on how long they last and where they come from:

  • Session cookies: last only while you're browsing, and clear when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies: stay on your device for a set period, helping us recognize you on return visits.
  • First-party cookies: set directly by our site, to support basic site features.
  • Third-party cookies: set by partners like Google AdSense and analytics tools, to support ads and analytics.
