Our Code of Ethics

The rules that keep our testing and reviews honest.

Our Commitment

Our goal is to give readers trustworthy, thoroughly checked reviews they can actually rely on.

Everyone who writes or tests for us follows this Code of Ethics. It's what keeps our reviews independent and free from outside commercial pressure.

1. Staying Objective

Every test, benchmark, and score is done as objectively as possible.

Independent analysis is at the core of what we do. Our testing is kept separate from marketing pitches and brand influence - no outside company or internal stakeholder can change or delay our results.

  • No special treatment for any brand: we avoid deals or agreements that would limit our ability to openly report a product's flaws.
  • Same conditions for every product: matching firmware versions, room temperature, and testing tools across the board.
  • Personal opinions stay out of the data: our scores are based on measurable results, not who our staff might personally know at a company.
  • Same standard for everyone: a small open-source app gets tested just as rigorously as software from a massive company.

2. Being Transparent

How we test, and how we make money, are both fully open to the public.

We share exactly how we reach our conclusions, so our work can be checked by anyone. Being open about our methods is what builds real trust.

  • Our test setup is public: benchmarking tools, test conditions, and configurations are listed openly so others could reproduce our results.
  • We clearly disclose how we make money - ads and affiliate links - and none of it affects our scores or what we write.
  • We keep our raw test data and recordings on file, so we can back up our findings if a manufacturer disputes them.
  • Our accounting team, who handles affiliate links, is kept separate from our writers and testers - so reviewers aren't even thinking about commissions while testing.

3. Getting the Facts Right

Every number, spec, and quote we publish needs to be accurate, checked against multiple sources, and backed by evidence.

Bad information hurts readers and our own reputation, so we take accuracy seriously. Our writers double-check claims to make sure marketing hype doesn't slip into a review as fact.

  • We don't publish results from a single test run - things like battery drain and temperature are measured multiple times to rule out flukes.
  • We clearly separate rumors and leaks from verified, hands-on test results, so readers always know what's confirmed and what's speculation.
  • If we get something wrong, we fix it right away and add a note explaining what changed and why.
  • We go back and update older articles when a major software update changes how a product performs, so our older reviews stay accurate.

4. Handling Confidential Information

Confidential prototypes, trade secrets, and any user data we collect are kept secure.

Sometimes we get early access to pre-release products or software. We take that responsibility seriously and never leak details ahead of an official release.

  • We honor NDAs and embargo dates - we don't break them for extra traffic.
  • Pre-release hardware is kept locked up securely in our testing space.
  • Any reader data we collect - feedback, emails, account details - is encrypted and handled according to standard privacy laws.

5. Being Accountable

We take full responsibility for everything we publish.

That means standing behind our work and actually listening when readers point out an error - not hiding behind excuses.

  • We regularly review old articles and update or archive them if a product is discontinued or a company changes how it operates.
  • We read and respond to feedback from readers and experts - it helps us catch gaps in our testing.
  • If someone reports a possible conflict of interest involving our team, management looks into it directly and resolves it quickly.

6. Following the Law

Everything we do - testing, publishing, business operations - follows applicable laws.

A trustworthy platform has to respect copyright, intellectual property, and fair competition. That's how we stay sustainable and respected long-term.

  • We respect copyright: we don't reuse others' content, images, or trademarks without permission.
  • We get our test units through legitimate means - buying them ourselves, official press loans, or public software releases.
  • Every ad and affiliate link follows standard advertising rules, and it's always clear to readers what's an ad versus what's editorial content.

A Note on Advertising

Ads on this site follow our safety and quality standards, and never affect our test results or scores.
