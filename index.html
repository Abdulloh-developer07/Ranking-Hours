How We Test at ranking-hours.uz

Finding a review you can actually trust is harder than it should be. A lot of tech sites just repeat what's in the press release and call it a review. We do things differently: we buy the products ourselves, use them the way a normal person would, and write down what actually happens - good or bad.

When a new phone, laptop, app, or car comes out, we don't just glance at the spec sheet. We use it for weeks. We check how the battery holds up over time, how it handles heat during heavy use, and where the build quality starts to show cracks - literally and figuratively. Every rating, chart, and comparison on this site comes from that hands-on process, not from a marketing deck.

We also keep our testing separate from our business side on purpose. The people running the tests don't deal with ads or partnerships, so there's no pressure to go easy on a brand. We buy our review units from regular stores, the same way you would, instead of accepting free samples that companies hand-pick to look good.

Tech doesn't stand still after launch day - a software update can make a great phone worse, or fix a laptop's early problems. So we don't just review something once and move on. We go back and re-check products after major updates, which is why you'll sometimes see a score change months after a product first came out.

Why Trust Our Reviews?

A few simple reasons our testing stays honest and useful.

We Buy Our Own Test Units

No free review samples picked by a PR team. We buy products from regular stores and use them like a normal customer would, so we catch the same flaws and quirks you'd run into.

Plain-Language Reviews

We skip the jargon and explain what the specs actually mean for daily use - what's genuinely useful, and where a product falls short in real life.

A Simple 5-Point Scale

Every product is scored the same way, across the same categories: reliability, value for money, build quality, and how well it fits into daily use. Same rules for every product, no favorites.

We Update Our Reviews

Products change after launch through software updates and fixes. When that happens, we go back, retest, and adjust the score if something's genuinely different.

How We Test Different Kinds of Products

For phones and other consumer gadgets, we go past the spec sheet. We check screen brightness in direct sunlight, see how hot the processor gets during gaming, and track real battery drain over normal daily use - not just a lab number, but what you'd actually get on a busy day.

For software - editing tools, design apps, cloud services - we look at how they hold up under real workloads. That means watching how much memory they use during heavy tasks, whether they crash on messy files, and how well they play with other tools you already use.

For cars and electric vehicles, we look at how the battery range holds up in both summer and winter, how the driver-assist features react to real road situations, and how fast charging actually is on different chargers. The goal is always the same: tell you what to expect before you spend the money, not after.
