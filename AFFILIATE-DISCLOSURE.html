Introduction

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, we may earn a small commission from the retailer - at no extra cost to you.

That commission helps cover the real costs of running this site - hosting, tools, and the products we buy to test.

It's what lets us keep the site free for readers while still doing proper, hands-on research.

We'd rather be upfront about this than hide it - you deserve to know how the site is funded.

Editorial Independence

Affiliate links never affect our ratings. A product either earns a good score or it doesn't - commissions have nothing to do with it.

Every review is based on actually using the product, not on which retailer pays the best commission.

Manufacturers and retailers have no say in what we publish. Paying us a commission doesn't buy a better review.

If a product has real problems, we'll say so, commission or not.

  • Being an affiliate partner doesn't guarantee a good review
  • Our editorial decisions stay independent
  • We value your trust more than short-term income
  • Our comparisons focus on real-world use, not spec sheets
  • We stay transparent about how this all works

Your trust matters more to us than squeezing out extra affiliate income. We're building this site to last, not to make a quick buck.

How Affiliate Links Work

Affiliate links carry a tracking code. If you click one and buy something afterward, the retailer pays us a small commission.

This never costs you anything extra - you pay the same price whether you use our link or go straight to the store.

Tracking usually works through a browser cookie, which tells the retailer the sale came from our site.

How long that tracking lasts depends on the retailer - anywhere from a few hours to several days after you click.

For more on cookies and your privacy, check our Cookie Policy and Privacy Policy pages.

Product Recommendations and Research

Our recommendations weigh things like specs, battery life, build quality, software support, and overall value for money.

We try to show you what actually matters day-to-day, not just numbers from a benchmark app.

Whether it's a phone, a laptop, headphones, or a car, we judge it by how it holds up in everyday use.

We're always working to make the site easier to read and use, on any device.

Our recommendations can change over time as new products come out or software updates change how something performs.

Commitment to Transparency

We think readers deserve to know how a site like this actually makes money.

So instead of hiding it, we're explaining it plainly here, while still focusing on making the content genuinely useful.

There's a lot of overly promotional junk out there, so we aim to keep our articles balanced and genuinely informative instead.

We'd rather build something readers trust long-term than chase a short-term traffic spike.

That's the plan going forward - keep improving the content and stay a site people can rely on.
