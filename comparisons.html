How We Verify Our Testing

Every test follows a consistent process to keep results fair. For phones and laptops, we start every device from the same thermal baseline before running benchmarks, so results aren't skewed by a device that's already warmed up.

For EVs, we go beyond the advertised range - checking battery degradation over time, real efficiency under load, and how responsive the driver-assist systems actually are.

Staying Neutral

We disclose our test parameters openly, and never adjust them to favor one brand's ecosystem over another.

That keeps our comparisons independent of manufacturer influence, whether we're testing an air purifier or a graphics card.
