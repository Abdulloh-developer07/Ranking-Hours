AirPods Pro 2 vs Sony WF-1000XM5: Which Wireless Earbuds Are Actually Better?

Why This Comparison Matters

Wireless earbuds aren't just for music anymore. In 2026, premium earbuds have become everyday tools for work calls, meetings, gaming, travel, workouts, and content creation.

The premium earbuds market right now really comes down to two options: Apple's AirPods Pro 2 and Sony's WF-1000XM5. Both are among the best noise-canceling earbuds you can buy, but they take pretty different approaches.

AirPods Pro leans into simplicity, comfort, and how smoothly it connects with everything else in the Apple ecosystem. Sony's WF-1000XM5 focuses more on raw sound quality, stronger noise cancellation, longer battery life, and deeper customization.

We tested both across phones, laptops, tablets, and gaming devices, listening to everything from hip-hop and jazz to podcasts and competitive gaming audio.

🏆 Overall Winner: Sony WF-1000XM5

Sony wins on sound quality, noise cancellation, and battery life. That said, AirPods Pro 2 is still the better pick for a lot of Apple users, mainly because of how well it fits into the Apple ecosystem and how comfortable it is to wear.

Sound Quality Comparison

Sound quality is probably the biggest factor for anyone spending this much on earbuds - you want rich bass, clear vocals, and a sense of space in what you're listening to.

AirPods Pro 2 has a clean, balanced sound. Vocals sound natural, podcasts come through clearly, and spatial audio adds a nice sense of depth for movies and supported tracks. Bass has improved over older AirPods models, but it's still a bit softer than Sony's.

The Sony WF-1000XM5 sounds noticeably more detailed and cinematic. Bass hits deeper, instrument separation feels more precise, and high frequencies stay smooth even during long listening sessions.

If you care a lot about sound staging and being able to fine-tune your EQ, you'll probably prefer Sony.

  • Sony delivers stronger bass and richer details
  • AirPods sound cleaner for podcasts and casual listening
  • Sony supports LDAC high-resolution audio codec
  • AirPods Pro works best inside the Apple ecosystem
  • Sony provides better audio customization tools

Active noise cancellation has become one of the most important features in premium earbuds - whether you're traveling, working in a loud office, or riding public transit, good ANC makes a real difference.

Active Noise Cancellation has become one of the most important features for modern wireless earbuds. Whether users are traveling, studying, working in noisy offices, or using public transportation, high-quality ANC dramatically improves the listening experience.

AirPods Pro 2 does a great job during daily commutes - it cuts down traffic noise, chatter, and general city sounds effectively, and Transparency mode is still one of the best around.

That said, Sony's WF-1000XM5 edges it out overall. During plane rides, subway trips, and even near construction noise, Sony consistently blocked more low-frequency sound.

Sony's microphones and processing just do a slightly better job creating quiet, especially while traveling.

Battery Life and Charging

Battery life matters a lot if you're commuting or working long hours - constantly needing to recharge gets old fast.

AirPods Pro 2 gets around 6 hours of listening with ANC on, and with the charging case, that stretches comfortably across a few days of normal use.

Sony does even better here - in our testing, the WF-1000XM5 consistently lasted around 8 hours with ANC on, making it one of the longest-lasting premium earbuds around.

Fast charging works well on both - a short charge gives you several extra hours of playback either way.

If battery life is a priority for you, especially for flights or long work days, Sony is the better pick.

Comfort and Everyday Usage

Comfort tends to get overlooked in reviews, but it matters a lot once you're wearing earbuds for hours at a time.

AirPods Pro 2 is still one of the most comfortable earbuds out there - they're light enough to basically forget you're wearing them, even during long sessions.

Sony's WF-1000XM5 is a real improvement over older Sony earbuds, but they're still a touch bigger and heavier than AirPods Pro.

During workouts, AirPods Pro felt a bit more secure. Sony held up fine too, but if you want the smallest, lightest option for the gym, Apple's design has the edge.

Final Verdict

After all this testing, the Sony WF-1000XM5 comes out ahead as the better overall pick for 2026 - better sound, stronger ANC, longer battery life, and more customization add up to a more premium experience.

That said, AirPods Pro 2 is still a fantastic choice if you're deep in the Apple ecosystem - the easy pairing, comfort, and call quality make it genuinely convenient for daily use.

If sound quality and noise cancellation are your top priorities, go with Sony. If you live in the Apple ecosystem and want something that just works, AirPods Pro 2 is the easier pick.

  • Choose Sony for sound quality and ANC
  • Choose AirPods for Apple ecosystem integration
  • Sony is better for audiophiles and travelers
  • AirPods are better for casual everyday convenience
  • Both earbuds remain among the best premium earbuds available today
