How Our Rating System Works

A plain explanation of how we turn hands-on testing into a final star rating.

1. Why We Built This System

A lot of tech reviews are just hype and gut feeling, sometimes shaped by marketing deals behind the scenes. That leaves buyers trying to decode marketing buzzwords instead of getting real, verified information.

We wanted a rating system based on actual test data, not gut feeling. We use consistent, repeatable tests and judge products on real functionality, durability, build quality, and long-term value - not packaging or marketing claims.

We use the same evaluation process for every category, whether it's a phone, an EV, or a home appliance. The final score isn't a vibe-based group decision - it's calculated from our weighted scoring formula.

2. Our 5-Star Scale

We use a 5-point scale, calculated to two decimal places, so products fall into five clear tiers:

5.0
★★★★★
Elite Class Exceptional

5.0 - Exceptional. Excels in every category with no notable flaws. A genuine standout in its class.

4.0
★★★★☆
Upper Core Class Highly Recommended

4.0-4.9 - Excellent. Strong marks across the board, with only minor compromises in non-essential features. A reliable, recommended choice.

3.0
★★★☆☆
Median Utility Class Satisfactory

3.0-3.9 - Solid but flawed. Does the core job reliably, but has clear trade-offs - like average build materials or so-so battery life. Worth considering mainly if discounted, or for a specific need.

2.0
★★☆☆☆
Sub-Standard Class Deficient

2.0-2.9 - Below average. Regular performance issues or design flaws that get in the way of normal use. Generally not recommended.

1.0
★☆☆☆☆
Defective Class Critical Alert

Under 2.0 - Poor. Fails at its core advertised job, or shows serious wear or safety concerns during testing. We'd recommend avoiding it.

This keeps scores meaningful - we save 5.0 for truly exceptional products, so a 4.0 or 4.5 still means a genuinely strong recommendation.

3. What Goes Into the Score

Every product is scored across seven categories, each weighted by how much it actually matters for daily use:

Performance & Core Power

Performance: raw speed, processing power, and how it handles heavy use.

Weight Alloc: 25%

Material Build & Rigidity

Build Quality: durability, materials, and long-term wear resistance.

Weight Alloc: 20%

Interface & Ergonomics

Ease of Use: setup, software, and how intuitive daily use is.

Weight Alloc: 15%

Pure Financial Value Index

Value for Money: how the price compares to what you actually get.

Weight Alloc: 15%

Consumption & Power Return

Battery & Efficiency: battery life, charging speed, and power draw.

Weight Alloc: 10%

Warranty & Service Support

Warranty & Support: coverage length, support quality, and parts availability.

Weight Alloc: 10%

Aesthetic & Industrial Vision

Design: how it looks, and whether the design actually serves its function.

Weight Alloc: 5%

This keeps scoring honest - if a product looks great but has poor battery life or overheats under load, those issues pull the overall score down even if the design score is high.

5. Common Questions

A few questions we hear often about how our scoring works:

A 100-point scale often creates a false sense of precision - the difference between 87 and 88 rarely means much. Our 5-point scale, tracked to two decimals, is precise enough to show real differences while staying grounded in actual test results.

We review our top-ranked products quarterly to account for software updates. If an update fixes a known issue or improves battery life, we retest and recalculate the score.

The category weights stay fixed, but what we measure within each one adapts to the product. For an appliance, "Performance" means power efficiency and noise; for a phone, it means processing speed and frame rates.

We don't do sponsored reviews or paid placements. Our team doesn't accept gifts or incentives from brands, which keeps our scores objective and focused on what's actually good for buyers.

Our Core Promise

We don't let affiliate links shape our recommendations, and we don't accept paid manufacturer partnerships. Every point in a score comes from actual hands-on testing.
