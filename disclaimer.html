The short version

Ranking Hours is a website that explains and compares products. We write general information to help you think through a purchase. We are not a shop, we do not sell anything ourselves, and we are not your lawyer, doctor, financial adviser, electrician or mechanic.

We work hard to be accurate and fair. But products change, prices move, and every reader's situation is different. So please treat our pages as a starting point for your research, check important facts with the maker or the seller, and make the final choice yourself.

The rest of this page explains these ideas in more detail, in plain language. It applies to every page on ranking-hours.uz. It should be read together with our Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Affiliate Disclosure.

General information only

Everything we publish is meant to inform. Our comparisons, guides and explanations describe products and topics in general terms. They are not instructions made for your exact situation, and they are not a promise that a certain product will work for you.

A guide about laptops cannot know which programs you run. A page about air conditioners cannot know the size and insulation of your room. A comparison of headphones cannot know the shape of your ears. We try to explain the points that matter so that you can apply them to your own case, but the final judgement has to be yours.

What this means for you: Use our pages to learn what to look for, then check the details of your own case before you buy.

We are not professional advisers

Some of our topics touch areas where professional advice matters. We want to be clear about the limits of what a website can do.

Money and purchases

We may talk about costs, value, subscriptions and savings. This is not financial advice. We do not know your income, your debts or your plans. Before a large purchase, a loan or an instalment plan, read the contract and think about whether you can afford it.

Law and contracts

We may mention warranties, return rights, licence terms or import rules. We are not lawyers, and rules differ between countries and change over time. If a legal question matters to you, ask a qualified person or the relevant authority.

Health and safety

Some pages cover products that affect your health or safety, such as headphones, chairs, car seats or kitchen appliances. We are not medical professionals. If you have a medical condition, a hearing problem or a concern about a product's effect on you, speak to a doctor.

Technical and professional work

We may describe how to build a PC, set up software or choose home equipment. Complex or dangerous work, like mains electrical wiring, gas appliances or car repairs, should be done by qualified people.

Accuracy and keeping up to date

We check our work and update it, but we cannot promise that every statement is complete, current and error free. Technology moves quickly. Phones get new software versions, laptops are refreshed every year, and cars and appliances are updated between model years.

Specifications also differ by region. The same product name can mean different hardware, accessories, warranty terms or software features depending on the country or the seller. A page written about one version may not describe the version in your local shop.

We show the date of the last update at the top of our pages. If something looks old, or if you notice a mistake, please tell us. We take corrections seriously and explain how we handle them on our corrections page.

What this means for you: Before you pay, check the exact model, the exact version and the current details on the maker or seller page.

Prices, deals and availability

We do not control prices, stock or promotions, because we do not sell products. Prices change daily and are not the same in every shop or country. For that reason, we usually avoid printing exact prices, and when we mention a price level, it is only a general guide.

A deal that looks great today may have ended. A product that we describe may be out of stock, or no longer sold, in your area. Some products are sold in Uzbekistan only through certain importers or with a local warranty that is different from the international one. Ask the seller what exactly is covered, and who handles repairs.

Prices in other currencies and the exchange rate can also shift. If you compare offers, compare the total cost, including delivery, taxes, accessories and any extra warranty.

What this means for you: Treat any price mentioned on this site as an example, not as an offer.

Opinions and conclusions

When we say a product suits a certain type of person, we are giving a reasoned opinion based on the information we looked at. Reasonable people can disagree. Your needs, budget, habits and tastes may lead you to a different choice, and that is fine.

We try to explain the reasoning behind each conclusion so you can see which priorities led to it. We do not claim that one product is the best for everybody. We also do not claim that a product we describe less favourably is bad. It may simply be a worse match for the use that a particular guide has in mind.

Safety

Many of the products we discuss involve electricity, batteries, heat, moving parts or vehicles. Please use them as the manufacturer describes, and follow the safety instructions in the manual.

  • Use the charger and cable that suit your device, from a reliable maker.
  • Do not use swollen, damaged or very hot batteries. Stop using them and get advice.
  • Have gas, water and mains electrical work done by qualified people.
  • Keep small parts and batteries away from young children.
  • Protect your hearing. Very loud sound through headphones can cause lasting damage.
  • Do not use a phone or other device in a way that distracts you while driving.

We cannot be responsible for harm that results from using a product in an unsafe way, or from following a general tip on our site without considering your own situation.

Warranty, returns and after-sales

Your warranty and return rights come from the seller, the maker and the law, not from us. We may describe what is typical, but the actual terms differ by product, seller and country.

When you buy, keep the receipt, the box and any warranty card. Ask what the warranty covers, how long it lasts, where service is done and whether it is valid in your country. For online purchases, check the return period and who pays for shipping.

If you have a problem with a purchase, contact the seller or the maker first. We are not able to handle claims or refunds, because we are not a party to your purchase.

Cars and road use

Our car comparisons look at published figures such as size, power, equipment and stated consumption or range. They are not a substitute for a test drive, an inspection or the maker's own documents.

Fuel use, electric range, acceleration and running costs depend on weather, road, driving style, load and the condition of the car. Official figures come from standard test cycles, and real life usually looks different. If you plan to buy a used car, have it checked by a trusted mechanic, and verify its history, papers and ownership with the proper authorities.

Safety ratings, equipment lists and available versions differ between markets. A car sold in one country may have different safety equipment from the one with the same name sold in another. Ask the seller for the exact specification of the exact vehicle you are looking at.

Home appliances and installation

When we compare fridges, washing machines, air conditioners, vacuum cleaners and similar products, we describe features, efficiency labels and typical use. We cannot see your home, your wiring or your water supply.

Check that a product suits your room size, voltage, space and plumbing before you buy. Some appliances need professional installation, such as air conditioners and built-in units. Poor installation can cause damage, reduce efficiency and make a warranty invalid. Always follow the manual, and use a qualified installer for anything that involves gas, high-power electrical connections or refrigerant.

Energy and water labels are useful for comparing products, but real consumption depends on how you use them. A larger family, hard water or a hot climate can change the results.
