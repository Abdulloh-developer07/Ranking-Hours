Understanding Frame Pacing vs Average FPS

It's worth knowing the difference between average frame rate and actual smoothness. A PC can show a high average FPS number and still feel stuttery if data isn't flowing smoothly between components - usually caused by mismatched memory speeds, slow storage, or a CPU running too hot and throttling itself.

That's why we pair fast DDR5 memory with fast NVMe storage in every build here - it keeps data moving quickly from your CPU to your GPU without any hiccups, which is what actually makes gameplay feel smooth, not just a high number on a benchmark chart.
